Gyuri Kang is a PhD student in Information Science at Indiana University Bloomington. She holds two master’s degrees in English and Information Science from University at Albany, SUNY, and a bachelor’s degree in English Language and Culture from Hanyang University in Korea. Her research lies in the digital environmental humanities, where she works at the intersection of humanistic inquiry into environmental issues and digital computing. She uses statistical tools and computational methods to answer humanistic questions about environmental problems of our age by looking at social and cultural relationship with the natural world mediated by texts, media, and history.
